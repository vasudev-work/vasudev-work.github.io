I build small Android apps that keep everything on your device — no account, no ads, no tracker, and no Google Play Services.

Most of that work ships as Nijatva, a suite of 24 single-purpose apps, and as the tooling and stores around it.

Nijatva, the suite GitHub LinkedIn Email

What I have built

Nine of the twenty-four Nijatva apps have a repository today; the other fifteen are planned and listed on the suite site. Outside the suite there are two other lines of work: a catalogue of Indian apps, and the small offline tools.

Main project

Nijatva Browser

An Android browser on the system WebView, about 9 MB: containers with their own tab sets, network and cosmetic filter rules applied on the device, reader mode, native text-to-speech, and a userscript manager. Desktop builds come from the same source.

in.nijatva.browserv2.0.3Kotlin

Nijatva Web Store

A script store for the browser, served as static files. The catalogue is signed with an ECDSA key and every script carries a SHA-256, so a file that changed under you is refused instead of installed.

GitHub Pagessigned catalogue

Nijatva Music

An offline player from one Compose Multiplatform codebase — Android, Windows, macOS, Linux and the browser. ID3 tags and cover art are parsed on the device, and the web build has a five-band equaliser on Web Audio and installs as a PWA.

Compose MultiplatformPWA

Camera and Photos

Camera2 with manual controls, RAW, night stacking, exposure fusion and panorama; and a local-first gallery with on-device AI, albums and a vault. Nothing is uploaded.

Camera2ML Kit

Phone roles

Dialer, Contacts, Messages, Files, Notes, Drive, Gallery, Video and Editor — the apps that make a de-Googled phone usable, each one small and offline.

MediaStoreSAFno INTERNET

Nijatva Translate

Translation for India's 22 scheduled languages against any LibreTranslate-compatible server, so the language data can stay on hardware you control.

Kotlin22 languages

Swadeshi

A catalogue of 1,502 Made-in-India Android apps with package ids and official links, published as CSV and JSON, plus an Android appstore app that lists them offline and a medicine page carrying 2,026 Jan Aushadhi generics.

dataAndroidstatic web

Small offline tools

Five apps — calculator, clock, notes, QR and files — around 115 KB each, with no INTERNET permission in the manifest at all. Also a privacy-first music player, two WebView readers with TTS and translation, and a multilingual AI assistant.

~115 KB eachno GMS

Build guards

no-gms-check is a Gradle plugin and a CI action that fails the build the moment a Google Play Services or Firebase artefact appears in the dependency tree — the rule the whole suite is held to, enforced by the compiler.

How I work

  • 1
    Offline first, and it stays offline Files are read from the device and written back to it. Where a feature needs the network, the page or app says so and the user chooses.
  • 2
    Small is a feature A browser of about 9 MB, apps of about 115 KB. The size is the argument, not a side effect.
  • 3
    Say what it does not do Every project page carries that section. A short honest list beats a long feature list nobody can check.
  • 4
    Make claims checkable Hashes on scripts, a signed catalogue, figures dated to the day they were read from GitHub. If a number can be verified, it is.
  • 5
    Built in India, for Indian networks Indian languages and slow connections are the first target, not an afterthought.
