Principles

Four rules we do not bend

Every app, without exception. If an app cannot meet all four, it does not ship.

No Google Play Services

Built for AOSP without GMS, so it runs on de-Googled Android and on BharOS.

No accounts

There is nothing to sign up for. Everything stays in device storage.

No ads or analytics

No tracking SDK is linked in. Not disabled, not opt-out. Absent.

Made in India

Indian languages and Indian networks as the first target, not an afterthought.

Install

APK, not a store

Releases are published on GitHub with the APK attached. Desktop builds of the Browser ship from the same pipeline. A self-hosted F-Droid style index comes with the suite repository.

The name

Why "Nijatva"?

In Sanskrit, nijatva means own-ness — the state of being your own. It is a good word for software that does not ask for your data in exchange for doing its job.

Nijatva is a project of DEVIRA, a complete IT company. The Browser ships releases today; the Camera is being built in the open; the rest of the twenty-four are planned, and this site says which is which.
