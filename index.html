Your phone. Your data.

Nijatva is a suite of 24 small, single-purpose Android apps. Each one does a job and then gets out of the way. No account, no ads, no tracker, no Google Play Services.

Android 7+ No Google Play Services BharOS and de-Googled ROMs Made in India

Four rules we do not bend

Every app, without exception

No Google Play Services

Every app is built for AOSP without GMS, so it runs on de-Googled Android and on BharOS.

No accounts

There is nothing to sign up for. Everything stays in your device's own storage.

No ads, no analytics

No tracking SDK is linked in. Not disabled, not opt-out. Absent.

Made in India

Built in India, with Indian languages and Indian networks as the first target, not an afterthought.

Why "Nijatva"?

In Sanskrit, nijatva means own-ness — the state of being your own. It is a good word for software that does not ask for your data in exchange for doing its job. Nijatva is a project of DEVIRA, a complete IT company. Apps are being released one at a time; source code follows each release.
